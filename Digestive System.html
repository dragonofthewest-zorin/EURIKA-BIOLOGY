DIGESTIVE SYSTEM

The Digestive System is the human body system that is responsible for digestion. Digestion is the process whereby complex food is broken down into simple, soluble and absorbable form.

The organs involved in Digestion are:

    Mouth(Boucal Cavity), Oesophagus, Stomach, Small Intestines, Large Intestines, Liver, Bile Gladder, Pancrease, Rectum, and Anus.

The Process of Digestion:

Mechanical Digesion: Mechanical Digestion also, chewing is when the mouth uses the teeth to physically breakdown to food into smaller pieces to form bollus.

Chemical Digestion: Chemical Digestion, is when the body uses digestive enzymes and the stomach acid to chemically breakdown the food usually changing the chemical composition of the food e.g Proteins-peptides and polypeptides and further,-amino acid

MOUTH: The mouth is the first organ in the process of digestion. Both mechananical and chemical digestion takes place in the mouth The mouth has the teeth and the tonge that chews(bite, crush, grind) and turn the food resepectively. This process is the mechanical digestion. The mouth also has three(3) salivery glands; The Submandibular gland, The Sublingual gland and The Paratoid. These glands, stores and releases saliver on the food. The saliver softens the food for easy swallowing. It aslo contains the enzyme, Salivary Amylase which breaks down carbohydrates in the food. This is the start of chemical digestion. The food is now turnd to bollus and swallowed.

OESOPHAGUS: The Oesophagus serves as the passage of food from the mouth, to the stomach. The food travels in the oesophagus through peristalsis. Peristalsis is the contraction and relaxation of the oesopagus to allow easy passage of food.
